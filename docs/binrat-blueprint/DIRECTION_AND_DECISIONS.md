# BINRAT — Accepted direction

**Owner-accepted:** overall product, hybrid visual experience, design-process and cheap-data direction, 2026-09-25 UTC. **Not approved:** exact CSS, typography, palette, Figma frames, library installation, paid plans or production actions.

## North star

**He gets the scraps. You get the receipts.** Robinhood Chain 4663 / Pons V2 comes first. BINRAT is a cheeky, clever, observant investigative rat: it discovers launches, verifies funding links, remembers comparable prior tokens, notices recurring buyers and can watch interesting changes. No wall of crypto jargon, fake confidence, recommendation score or obligatory conversation with a bot just to view a token.

The target is an impatient memecoin user: understand a meaningful finding in ~5 seconds, inspect a story in ~30 seconds, follow with one clear action. Real raw receipts and a deeper expert view remain accessible.

## Four product surfaces

- **Fresh Garbage:** the expressive homepage and new Pons launch stories. Large real rat artwork on home, each finding brief and engaging.
- **Dig Deeper:** focused token dossier, observed lifecycle and phase, trading, creator roles, liquidity/exit, prior launches, citations; rat becomes smaller and restrained.
- **Rat Trap:** repeat funder, direct and justified deeper funding paths, previous funded launches, observed outcomes, recurring buyer evidence, historical temperature and selective funding diagram.
- **Rat Watch:** selected wallet/launcher/token alerts on site and Telegram from the **same evidence-derived findings**, deduplicated and cost-capped with honest freshness/delivery status.

Additional investigated capabilities: Funding Trail, Familiar Faces, Early Wallets, Exit Door, Burn History, Holder Movement, Graduation Watch, Creator Economics, Trade Activity and Shared Funding. A planned feature is never automatically a live capability.

## Visual and interaction direction

**Hybrid: the rat's world for discovery; a professional, fast evidence tool for investigation.** Explore Dumpster Workshop, Underground Intelligence Bureau and Living Case File as three variations **inside the hybrid**. Compare the same frozen data and complete home → investigation → Rat Trap journey on mobile and desktop. No final art treatment or style guide until the owner approves a coherent experience across screens. Avoid generic themed dashboard, bubble-map default and neon-card soup. Evidence graphs unfold only where actual relationships add meaning. Pixelated approved rat must not be redesigned; PixiJS and Rive are implementation experiments, not permission to alter the mascot.

## Proposed frontend and design methods

**Leading candidate:** Svelte 5 + SvelteKit + TypeScript, contingent on a working prototype; React + TypeScript remains fallback. Figma is the accepted visual exploration and approval workspace. Candidate supporting tools: unstyled Bits UI, Storybook component specimens, Svelte Flow for bounded verified funding diagrams, ECharts for historical data, optional PixiJS rat/environment, Playwright + axe-core for functional/visual/a11y checks. Only load graphics and analytical bundles on relevant screens.

**Creative freedom:** high during disposable concept exploration, moderate refining an owner-selected prototype, bounded when implementing additional screens with approved components; **zero** latitude to invent on-chain evidence. Establish contextual design decision guides from expert principles, but allow specific, tested exceptions. Extract tokens and reusable components only from approved working examples rather than generating speculative visual law.

## Cheap and sustainable research

Collect a source fact once, verify it, derive reusable intelligence and publish the same factual finding to site/Telegram. Limit normal collection to verified Pons factory, known curves, relevant launch-wallet funding and verified graduated V4 pools. Public RPC/free explorer APIs are research candidates; per-source completeness and costs must be measured. Existing TypeScript/viem + SQLite and exploratory DuckDB/Parquet/R2 are candidates; MCP is primarily for exceptional human/agent research, never unbounded production access.

Free: truthful launch discovery, public receipts, bounded history and watch plus basic thermometer when proven. Paid later: more watch slots, custom research, larger historical cohorts, exports, optional improved delivery only where measured. $19/$29 are pricing hypotheses. No token fees required for solvency. Payment and token-holder benefits need separate legal, technical and owner gates.

**Professional success** means distinctive but cohesive visuals, swift comprehension, useful research, mobile quality, accessibility, reproducible honest facts, and zero silent style drift across LLM handoffs.

# Hybrid UX and Figma visual exploration brief

**Accepted:** Figma will be the design exploration and owner-approval workspace. **Not yet accepted:** a final visual identity, screenshot, CSS system or Figma file. This document may be applied to a newly created, separately approved Figma file. Do not select a visual direction by resurrecting the deleted historical frontend.

## One creative brief, three hybrid studies

**World:** a mischievous, clever, dumpster-intelligence rat investigating genuine Pons V2 launches on Robinhood Chain 4663. The same approved low-resolution pixel-art rat master must preserve face, ear silhouette, cyber-eye placement, palette and attitude. A master reference must be located and checked before importing or commissioning additional states. Do not redraw or invent a generic rat to fill the gap.

**Shared interaction across all three studies:** Fresh Garbage discovery -> open one token -> inspect verified and unknown funding -> compare previous launches + thermometer -> expand useful wallet trail -> enable a Rat Trap. All variants use identical realistic **frozen example data**; genuine Pons #40 receipts can anchor actual launch facts, while hypothetical funded/price outcomes must be conspicuously marked DEMO. None may silently claim live funding intelligence.

Three studies, all genuinely hybrid:
1. **Dumpster Workshop:** expressive cluttered HQ for discovery, environmental scanner and rat animations; calm case-file workspace for research. Risk: game scenery displaces token utility.
2. **Underground Intelligence Bureau:** gritty analogue investigative command room, crisp meaningful instrumentation and eccentric rat operator; serious dossier view. Risk: generic trading-terminal aesthetic.
3. **Living Case File:** transaction receipts, evidence boards, dossiers, timeline and incremental discoveries; rat as finder and commentator. Risk: ornamental investigation slowing repeat use.

Avoid stitching every favored part together indiscriminately; approve **one coherent complete design** and selectively iterate within it. The world is an inspiration, not authorization for purple/neon/CRT/bento/cyberpunk CSS lock-in.

## Rat scale and interaction grammar

- **Homepage:** authoritative rat is prominent, lively and visually unmistakable. Discovery is immediate, accessible text remains HTML rather than embedded in sprite/canvas.
- **Dig Deeper:** rat is a restrained guide, the token finding and facts are the visual center. Long transactions and charts go under progressive disclosure, not a giant first-screen dashboard.
- **Rat Trap:** rat's narrative introduces documented funding; a small justified relationship widget *unfolds as a reward* only when the evidence forms something worth seeing. Default to an accessible chronological trail; no arbitrary Sigma/bubble-map soup.
- **Rat Watch:** conspicuous but nonintrusive actionable controls with observable success, disabled and error states. Persistent selected alerts, no dummy button pretending live monitoring.
- **Social/creator links:** clearly distinguish external metadata links from on-chain verified source; do not visually overclaim identity.

## Professional default checks without locked aesthetics

First visual question: what is the user's job on this screen? Second: what deserves immediate attention? Third: which action is next and what will it do? Fourth: what should be grouped and hidden until requested? Fifth: does the page work with real content and touch/keyboard input?

Use a clear dominant element, one legible main reading route, meaningful grouping and hierarchy, obvious navigation, useful whitespace and adequate touch regions. **These are reasoning defaults, not mandates** about one hero geometry, fixed grid, spacing ratio, font or visual effect. Deliberate asymmetry and carefully justified convention-breaking remain encouraged. Error/unknown states receive the same craft as the happy path.

**Mobile:** capture and review 320, 360, 390 and 430 CSS-px viewports, plus a selected desktop and intermediate viewport. Ensure legible copy, no horizontal overflow, targets/focus, useful hierarchy, acceptable loading/graphics and reduced-motion support. Explore animated mascot as pixel-faithful sprites or conditional PixiJS. Rive is an optional experiment only if approved art can be translated without drifting from the master and mobile performance survives.

## Owner approval gate

Each of the three variants must demonstrate the full comparable journey (not just a hero), important evidence gaps, one 6h/24h/3d/7d historical selector, real accessible actions, coherent home/detail modes and mobile. Present side-by-side Figma frames and interaction prototype plus screenshots from disposable Svelte proof when needed.

After selecting one coherent visual treatment, document only its essential visual principles; extract component specimens, tokens and motion rules from **working approved screens**. Never treat screenshots/tests as proof the initial design was attractive: initial approval is a separate owner decision. Record approved exceptional new component treatment as a dated addition rather than stealth global restyling.

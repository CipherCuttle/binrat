# BINRAT PHILOSOPHY V0

Status: product doctrine / early fair-launch commitment  
Base product: Intelligence V1 at `69793aef3ac6c152e0a91d8c914a6f71c5a4f0f3`

## The rat remembers

Crypto launches are optimized for immediacy. Websites disappear, social accounts change, creator wallets rotate, narratives are rewritten, and most tooling focuses on what is happening now.

BINRAT exists to preserve what happened, what was observable at the time, and how the current launch relates to earlier evidence.

**He gets the scraps. You get the receipts.**

## Permanent principles

### 1. Memory beats prediction

BINRAT's durable advantage is longitudinal memory: launches, creator history, point-in-time observations, public artifacts, relationships, and replayable receipts.

Prediction may be layered on top by users and researchers. BINRAT's authority remains the underlying evidence.

### 2. Receipts beat scores

BINRAT should prefer:

- "this ArcPad-reported creator address appeared in 7 indexed launches";
- "this domain was observed at launch and later disappeared";
- "these 11 observable launch characteristics match these prior launches";
- "coverage is PARTIAL";

over:

- "SAFE 82/100";
- "RUG";
- "GOOD BUY";
- unsupported identity claims.

A user should be able to inspect why a statement exists.

### 3. Missing evidence is not good evidence

Unavailable, immature, unsupported, contradictory, or incomplete evidence remains visibly unknown, partial, disputed, or unverified.

Silence never becomes safety.

### 4. Point-in-time integrity

BINRAT must be able to answer:

> What could BINRAT have known at this moment?

Future observations must never leak into earlier views, replays, heuristics, or evaluations.

### 5. Wealth does not buy truth

Future token ownership, staking weight, or bounty size must never decide factual truth.

Economic stake may provide Sybil resistance and punish spam. Evidence decides evidence.

### 6. The product must survive the token going to zero

Core indexing, receipts, Creator Files, Trash Trails, historical replay, and public evidence must remain truthful regardless of the market price of `$BINRAT`.

The token can fund, coordinate, gate optional features, and create a degen/community layer. It does not own evidence authority.

### 7. Launch early, build utility in public

BINRAT intends to fair-launch `$BINRAT` early in the project rather than wait until every roadmap utility exists.

The reason is explicit: the launch is intended to help bankroll continued development while giving the community a native coordination/culture asset from the beginning.

Fair-launch doctrine:

- no private presale or discounted insider round;
- no hidden team allocation;
- no promise of returns or future token value;
- launch mechanics, creator/project fees, any founder purchase, and treasury wallets are disclosed;
- project funding from token-related fees is tracked publicly;
- roadmap utility is labeled SHIPPED, BUILDING, PLANNED, or EXPERIMENTAL.

The token should become more useful as BINRAT ships, but roadmap utility is a product commitment, not a price claim.

### 8. Rat Credits are earned reputation-adjacent coordination units

Rat Credits remain off-chain and non-transferable.

They are earned or spent through product activity such as:

- evidence contribution;
- Trash Hunts;
- bounties;
- challenges;
- proof-of-first;
- anti-spam bonds;
- useful community work.

Rat Credits are not equity, yield, or an automatic claim on `$BINRAT`.

### 9. Reputation is earned, not bought

Contributor reputation is non-transferable and evidence-derived.

Useful dimensions may include:

- accepted submissions;
- overturned submissions;
- successful challenges;
- evidence categories;
- completeness;
- duplicate/spam rate;
- unresolved claims.

A wealthy account with bad evidence should be less trusted than a small account with a strong record.

### 10. Creators retain a right of reply, not a right to rewrite history

A creator may prove control of an address and attach signed context.

That context is clearly labeled as a creator-signed claim.

It never deletes or rewrites prior receipts.

### 11. Automation must remain attributable

Bots, researchers, indexers, models, and humans may contribute evidence in the future.

Every contribution must remain attributable to a source, time, method, evidence bundle, and current status.

No model-generated statement becomes evidence merely because a model produced it.

## Product identity

BINRAT is not trying to become another generic trading terminal.

The intended identity is:

> **Git history for crypto launch provenance.**

Wallets change. Sites vanish. Accounts rename. Stories change.

The rat remembers.

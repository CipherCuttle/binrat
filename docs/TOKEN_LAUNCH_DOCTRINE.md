## Intent

BINRAT intends to launch $BINRAT early enough that product, culture and utility can grow together.

The token is not presented as proof that a mature network already exists.

Launch V1 has three product reasons:

1. **culture / coordination** — give the emerging Rat workforce a native community asset;
2. **product access** — verified active stake can unlock more bounded Rat labor/capacity;
3. **build in public** — ship utility progressively against inspectable capability state.

None of these is a promise of token appreciation, income, APY or future return.

## Fair launch means fair access and disclosed mechanics

Launch V1 target:

- no private presale;
- no discounted insider round;
- no hidden team allocation;
- one public market;
- public contract facts after verification;
- disclosed launch mechanics;
- disclosed staking-vault route and dependency risk;
- disclosed founder/project purchases, if any;
- disclosed treasury/role wallets where applicable;
- no fake “community launch” while privileged inventory is hidden.

Current owner-selected policy:

- creator tax: **0%**;
- opening/dev buy: **0 ETH**;
- any later founder/project purchase: ordinary public market only and disclosed.

Fair does not mean risk-free. It means the important mechanics are not hidden.

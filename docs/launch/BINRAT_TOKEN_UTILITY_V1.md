# BINRAT token utility V1

`$BINRAT = capacity in the BINRAT intelligence network.`

BINRAT remains useful without a token. Public factual receipts, DIG and WHY do not become more or less true for a holder: facts remain identical to FREE. The token may later change convenience/capacity only: more Watch capacity, advanced `/rats`, deeper investigations, greater alert/research capacity, future Rat Den group capacity, and future Deep Dig/custom detector capacity.

The neutral policy categories are `watchLimit`, `digQuota`, `historyDepth`, `ratsFilters`, `alertCapacity`, `groupCapacity`, `deepDigAllowance`, and `customDetectorAllowance`. Config supports `FREE`, `PRO`, `HOLDER_TIER_1`, `HOLDER_TIER_2`, and optional `HOLDER_TIER_3`; no non-FREE tier is active now.

No final threshold exists until the token, decimals/supply, distribution, costs, holder probe, finality/reorg policy and owner approval are all verified. No yield, APR, profit, price appreciation, permanent access, preferential factual result, or gambling mechanic is promised.

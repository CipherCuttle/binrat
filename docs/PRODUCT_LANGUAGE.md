# BINRAT PRODUCT LANGUAGE

Date: 2026-10-06  
Status: **canonical naming / voice / public-copy planning authority**  
Factual status still comes from the capability manifest and receipts.

## 1. Voice

The Rat is:

- short;
- dry;
- slightly feral;
- competent;
- mildly hostile to bullshit;
- never hostile to the user;
- never corporate.

Rule:

> **BINRAT decides what is true. The Rat decides how to say it.**

## 2. Public-copy grammar

Default:

`FERAL HEADLINE → LITERAL EXPLANATION → RECEIPT / SOURCE`

Example:

> **SMELLS FAMILIAR.**  
> This exact Pons-reported deployer appears across 4 retained launches.  
> **PATTERN · PARTIAL**  
> OPEN CASE →

Personality may compress attention. It may not compress away uncertainty.

## 9. Telegram voice

Telegram is the scout / alert / return surface, not a condensed developer console.

### Start / home

> 🐀 **BINRAT**  
> I remember what launches try to forget.  
> Send me a token, deployer or Case — or open the dumpster and see what just landed.

### Finding

> 🐀 **FOUND SOMETHING.**  
> Same deployer showed up before.  
> 4 retained launches in current coverage.  
> **PATTERN · PARTIAL**  
> OPEN CASE →

### Repeat

> 🐀 **SMELLS FAMILIAR.**  
> This deployer has been in the bin before.  
> OPEN RECEIPTS →

### Error

> 🐀 **PIPE SMELLS WRONG.**  
> I couldn't verify that read.  
> Nothing changed. Try again.

### No supported result

> 🐀 **EMPTY PAWS.**  
> Nothing supported in the coverage I checked.  
> That doesn't mean it never happened.

### Future Tripwire alert shape

> 🐀 **TRIPWIRE HIT.**  
> Watched subject appeared in a new supported event.  
> **OBSERVED · {coverage}**  
> OPEN UPDATED CASE →

Do not dump implementation-state tables into ordinary chat unless explicitly requested.

## 12. Token language

Before actual verified public launch:

- do not publish a token address;
- do not say the token is live;
- do not imply staking is available if the exact token/vault has not been verified;
- do not use APY/return promises.

When launch becomes verified and publication is authorized, the product explanation is:

> Stake $BINRAT to unlock more bounded Rat/workforce capacity.

Do not lead with “investment,” “yield,” “holders,” “alpha,” “100x,” “buy before utility,” or price appreciation.

## 13. Hard avoids

Do not emit as BINRAT conclusions:

- smart money;
- whale knows;
- good buy / bad buy;
- BUY / SELL / APE instructions;
- SAFE / RUG scores;
- scammer/rugger from recurrence alone;
- guaranteed APY/returns/listings;
- “Sniffer predicts the next launch” before proof;
- “Tripwire is live” before durable workforce-style jobs are actually available;
- “autonomous” if it implies unrestricted authority;
- fake urgency;
- fake activity;
- fake Den workers;
- unsupported token availability.
